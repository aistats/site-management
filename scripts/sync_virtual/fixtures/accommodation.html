Hotel Information & Reservations

AISTATS 2026 will take place at the Hilton Tangier Al Houara Resort & Spa in Tangier, Morocco. The conference dates are May 2–6, 2026, and the hotel blocks are set for May 1–6, 2026.

Important booking note: Rooms booked through the official AISTATS hotel blocks should generally be around 150–200 USD per night. If you are seeing rates significantly above that, you are likely not booking through our conference block correctly.

Main hotel booking update

The main conference hotel, Hilton Tangier Al Houara Resort & Spa, has reached booking capacity for the discounted conference block.

The hotel may still have rooms available, but if so, they may appear at standard public rates rather than our conference block pricing.

Please use one of the official overflow hotel booking links below. Transportation from each overflow hotel to the main conference hotel is provided.

Overflow hotel booking links

Expected conference block pricing: approximately $160–$200 USD per night. If the rate you see is materially higher, please double check that you are using the correct booking link below.
